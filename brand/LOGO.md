# OneUp Logo — source of truth

> Simple mark. Story: **listen · understand · +1 growth**.  
> Files: `brand/logos/*.svg` · Showcase: `prototype/oneup-brand/logo.html`

---

## Story → shape

| Story beat | Visual |
|------------|--------|
| **Listen** | Soft open circle / arc (ear / attention) |
| **Understand** | Quiet center · nested calm |
| **+1 / OneUp** | Clear plus + one — next step |
| **Develop** | Slight ascent or orbit offset — not stairs clutter |

**Rule:** One focal idea. Thick enough to feel solid (stroke ~3.5–4.5 / 100). ≥40% empty space. Black / white only.

**Boot / launch:** Pair Bond stroke-draw story — arc → pair → +/1 → wordmark (~3.2s, skippable). Beat labels: Listen · Understand · Your +1.  
**Chrome loader:** `omi-load.svg` — Seed Omi idle smile + dual orbit rings (`#ouLoad`, tab switch).  
**Ask wait:** inline chat thinking (avatar mood + bubble) — **not** this mark. See `pages/onboard.md` · `pages/coach.md`.

## Recommended lock

**`05-pair-plus.svg` — Pair +Bond +1**  
Open circle = listening and growth. Two points = user + Omi. The connected
plus shares its vertical with a stylized `1` (lengthened flag/nose) = one understood next step.

Alt: **`03-listen-plus-one.svg`** — open arc cradling a literal +1.

**Chrome loader (not App Icon, not Ask):** `omi-load.svg` — Seed idle smile + concentric open rings (orbit). `#ouLoad` only.

## iOS app icon

The production app uses Pair +Bond +1 with an opaque, full-bleed background.
iOS applies the platform corner mask; do not round the source artwork.

- Light master: `logos/app-icon-pair-bond-light.svg`
- Dark master: `logos/app-icon-pair-bond-dark.svg`
- PNG exports: `app-icon-exports/{light,dark}/`
- Export sizes: 20, 29, 40, 58, 60, 76, 80, 87, 120, 152, 167, 180, 512, 1024 px
- Xcode source: `OneUp/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`
- Safe area: mark occupies 75% of the 1024 px canvas
- PNG rule: RGB, opaque, no alpha channel
